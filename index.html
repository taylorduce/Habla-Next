What this is. Keep this page open while you try to speak Spanish. It listens through the phone mic — or AirPods, if those are the iPhone’s current microphone — and guesses the next word plus a few full sentence endings.

How to use it. Add this page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari). Tap Listen, grant the microphone, start talking. Glance at the chips when you stall. Tap a word to lock it in; the model then predicts from there. After 3 seconds of silence the transcript and predictions start a new sentence. Stopping, then tapping Listen again, also starts a fresh prediction.

For your fiancé. There is a matching English-learning app in the en folder — Spanish labels, English listening, Spanish translations under each guess.

Learning. Every finished phrase is stored only on this device. Predictions marked “from your speech” come from your own patterns, blended with a built-in conversational Spanish model. Export the data file if you switch phones.

Limits. This is a web app, not an App Store app. iOS will stop listening if you lock the phone or switch away. Safari speech recognition can pause after silence; the app restarts it. It cannot hear you in another app at the same time. Predictions start useful immediately and get more “you” after a few days of talking into it.

Personal speech data stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.
